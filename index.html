Agent systems that do real work.

Orthogon is an applied AI lab in New York, run by Noah Freedman. It builds multi-agent systems and puts them to work inside real businesses — where the paperwork is binding and the content actually gets published.

The house rule is orthogonality: generation ⊥ verification — the agent that writes never grades its own work. Every claim has to survive a check it didn't write, which is why the checking tools are the part that's public.
